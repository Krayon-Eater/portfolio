Who am i?

Designer, Artist, Athlete

My name is Keegan Ouillette, im a Michigander born and raised. My imagination and ambition have always been my greatest strengths. Be warned if you get me hooked on an idea, then you can almost guarantee I will be awake into the wee hours of the night. Apart from my creativity, I also enjoy all-things outdoors, sports, and cooking.

I come from a large and loving family who've always supported my endeavors. They always say "it takes a village" and that couldn't be more true. The freedom of small town living is truly a blessed place for raising children. Until I graduated from highschool, I was consistently involved in community events and team sports.

It was in highschool that I found the opportunity to use my talent in a benefitial way. I joined my highschools' publications team as a designer. I helped create advertisement posters for fundraisers and helped with the schools sports photography. But out of all that, the most remarkable memory was when I designed my teams' jersey when we travelled to Wisconson.

Since then, I've been working towards my Graphic Design BFA at Central Michigan University, MI. I've been able to explore and expand not just my knowledge but my experience too. In my most recent project, I've been working on a complete brand design for art district, inlcuding merch, marketing, and campiagn strategies.
