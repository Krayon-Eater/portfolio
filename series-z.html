Series Z:
Roronoa Zoro


Series Z is a collection of inspired artworks of the fictional character Roronoa Zoro, from the manga and anime (and now live action) One Piece, created by the legendary author Eiichiro Oda. With each artwork demonstrating either a peronsality or aesthetic value derived from Zoro, I wanted to capture all aspects of the character. I think Zoro is the fucking best of all the Strawhat crew, and will become the World's Greatest Swordsman and his name will reach to the heavens to let Kuina know.

In this project, I wanted to explore the character of Zoro and his various traits and characteristics. I wanted to create a collection of artworks that would capture the essence of Zoro and his journey to become the World's Greatest Swordsman. I also wanted to create a collection of merchandise that would allow fans of Zoro to show their love for the character in a unique and stylish way.
