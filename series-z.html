Series Z: Zoro Roronoa

Series Z is a collection of geometric portraits of the characters from the popular anime and manga series, One Piece. The project was inspired by my love for the series and my interest in geometric art. Each portrait is created using a combination of circles, triangles, and rectangles to capture the essence of the character while maintaining a minimalist aesthetic.
